# Mandi Prices and Commodity Rates in Karnataka

## Market Rates (Kolar, Hubballi, Bengaluru APMC Mandis)
- **Tomato:** Modal Price ₹1,800 to ₹2,400 per quintal in Kolar APMC.
- **Onion:** Modal Price ₹1,600 to ₹2,200/quintal in Hubballi mandi.
- **Maize:** Modal Price ₹2,100/quintal.
- **Green Chilli:** Modal Price ₹3,200/quintal.

## Advisory
Tomato prices in Kolar APMC fluctuate based on daily supply from local clusters. Ensure grading into A, B, C quality bins before bringing produce to market for better bargaining.
