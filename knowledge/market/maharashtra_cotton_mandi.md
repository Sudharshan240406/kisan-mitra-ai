# Maharashtra Cotton & Agricultural Mandi Price Guide

## Overview
Market price benchmarks for major agricultural commodities across Maharashtra mandis (Yavatmal, Nagpur, Nashik, Pune).

## Commodity Prices (Agmarknet 2026 Reference)
- **Cotton (Long Staple)**:
  - Yavatmal APMC: ₹6,850 - ₹7,200 per quintal (Modal: ₹7,000/quintal)
  - Nagpur APMC: ₹6,800 - ₹7,150 per quintal (Modal: ₹6,950/quintal)
- **Soybean**:
  - Latur APMC: ₹4,450 - ₹4,600 per quintal
- **Onion**:
  - Lasalgaon APMC: ₹2,100 - ₹2,300 per quintal

## Advisory for Farmers
- Cotton prices in Maharashtra mandis remain strong due to steady textile mill demand.
- Farmers are advised to dry cotton to below 8% moisture before bringing produce to APMC yards to fetch maximum modal price.
