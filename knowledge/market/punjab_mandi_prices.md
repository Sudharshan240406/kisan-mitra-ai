# Mandi Prices and Commodity Rates in Punjab

## Current Mandi Rate Trends (Punjab Mandis: Ludhiana, Khanna, Jalandhar)
- **Wheat (Kanak):** Modal Price ₹2,275 to ₹2,350 per quintal. Minimum Support Price (MSP): ₹2,275/quintal. Arrival volume is high.
- **Paddy / Rice (PBR 1121 & Basmati):** Basmati modal price ₹3,800 - ₹4,200/quintal; PR-126 paddy ₹2,320/quintal.
- **Maize:** Modal Price ₹2,050/quintal.
- **Cotton:** Modal Price ₹6,800 to ₹7,200/quintal.

## Advisory for Farmers
Prices in Ludhiana and Khanna mandis remain stable near MSP. Selling dry grain with moisture below 12% ensures maximum procurement price without dockage.
