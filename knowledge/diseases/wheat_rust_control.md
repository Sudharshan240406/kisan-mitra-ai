# Wheat Rust Disease Pathology and Management Guide

## Disease Identification
- **Yellow / Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis):** Yellow pustules arranged in linear stripes on wheat leaves. Prevalent in cooler humid regions of Punjab and Haryana.
- **Brown / Leaf Rust (Puccinia triticina):** Oval orange-brown pustules scattered randomly on leaf surfaces.

## Treatment and Control Advisory
1. **Chemical Spray:** Apply Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/litre of water (500 ml in 200 L water per acre) or Tebuconazole 250 EC @ 1 ml/L upon initial appearance of pustules.
2. **Repeat Spray:** Repeat spray after 15 days if disease severity persists.
3. **Resistant Varieties:** Plant certified rust-resistant wheat varieties such as HD-3086, DBW-187, or HD-3226.
