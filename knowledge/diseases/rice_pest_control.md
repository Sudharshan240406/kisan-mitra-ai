# Rice Crop Pathology & Pest Control Guide

## Overview
Diagnostic and treatment guide for major paddy/rice crop diseases across South and Central India.

## Common Diseases & Treatments

### 1. Rice Blast (Pyricularia oryzae)
- **Symptoms**: Spindle-shaped lesions on leaves with grey/white centers and reddish-brown margins.
- **Treatment**: Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6g/L water or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5ml/L water at first sign of leaf blast.

### 2. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH)
- **Symptoms**: Drying of plants in circular patches ("hopper burn").
- **Treatment**: Spray Pymetrozine 50% WDG @ 0.6g/L or Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 0.4g/L at base of plants.

### 3. Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked lesions turning yellow-white with wavy margins along leaf tips.
- **Treatment**: Spray Streptocycline @ 0.1g/L + Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5g/L water.
