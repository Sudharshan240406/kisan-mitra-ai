# Tomato Brown Spot and Early Blight Management

## Disease Symptoms
- **Early Blight (Alternaria solani):** Dark brown spots with concentric target-like rings on lower leaves, surrounded by yellow chlorotic halos.
- **Septoria Leaf Spot:** Small circular gray-brown spots with dark borders.

## Recommended Control Actions
1. **Fungicide Application:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 3 g/L at 10-14 day intervals.
2. **Systemic Fungicide for Severe Infection:** Spray Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 ml/L.
3. **Cultural Practices:** Remove infected lower leaves, avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation, maintain proper plant spacing for air circulation, and mulch soil around plant base.
