# Weather and Rain Advisory for Punjab & North India

## Meteorological Forecast
- **Region:** Ludhiana, Amritsar, Jalandhar, Punjab & Haryana Plains.
- **Weekly Rainfall Outlook:** Moderate to heavy rainfall (25mm - 65mm) expected over the next 5-7 days due to active monsoon trough.
- **Temperature & Humidity:** Max temp 30°C - 33°C, relative humidity 75% - 88%.

## Agronomic Advice
1. **Irrigation Pause:** Hold irrigation for wheat and paddy fields to prevent waterlogging and nitrogen leaching.
2. **Drainage:** Keep field channels open for excess rainwater drainage.
3. **Fungicide/Pesticide Warning:** Avoid spraying chemicals right before rainfall. Re-apply spray if rain occurs within 2 hours of application.
