# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Scheme Guide

## Scheme Overview
PM-KISAN provides direct income support of ₹6,000 per year to small and landholding farmer families across India. Payment is transferred directly into bank accounts in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months.

## Eligibility Criteria
- All landholding farmer families with cultivable landholding in their names.
- **Exclusions:** Institutional landholders, former/present constitutional office holders, government employees, and income tax payers.

## How to Apply & Required Documents
1. **Documents Needed:** Aadhaar Card, Bank Account linked with Aadhaar, Landholding Records (Khasra/Khatauni/Patta).
2. **Application:** Apply via PM-KISAN Portal (pmkisan.gov.in) or nearest CSC center. Mandatory eKYC must be completed via OTP or biometric authentication.
