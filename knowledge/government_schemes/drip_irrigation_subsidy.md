# Micro Irrigation & Drip Subsidy Scheme (Per Drop More Crop)

## Scheme Details
Under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) - Per Drop More Crop component, financial assistance is provided to farmers for adopting drip and sprinkler irrigation systems.

## Subsidy Breakdown
- **Small & Marginal Farmers:** Up to 55% financial subsidy on total drip system cost.
- **Other Farmers:** Up to 45% financial subsidy.
- Additional top-up subsidies are provided by state governments (e.g., Karnataka, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh).

## Required Documents & Process
- Land ownership certificate (RTC / Patta), Aadhaar card, Bank passbook, soil and water test report.
- Submit application through state agriculture/horticulture department portal or district horticulture officer.
