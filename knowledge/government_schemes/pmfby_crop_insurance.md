# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Crop Insurance Guide

## Overview
PMFBY provides comprehensive crop insurance cover against yield losses caused by natural non-preventable risks (drought, flood, pest attack, unseasonal rain).

## Premium Rates for Farmers
- **Kharif Crops:** 2.0% of sum insured.
- **Rabi Crops:** 1.5% of sum insured.
- **Commercial & Horticultural Crops:** 5.0% of sum insured.
- Remaining premium subsidy is shared 50:50 by Central and State Governments.

## Claim Process
- In case of localized calamity or post-harvest loss, intimation must be given within 72 hours via PMFBY App, Toll-Free Number, or Bank/Insurance office.
