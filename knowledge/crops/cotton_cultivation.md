# Cotton Cultivation & Agronomy Guide

## Overview
Cotton is a major commercial kharif crop grown extensively in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Telangana, and Karnataka.

## Soil & Climate Requirements
- Requires deep black cotton soil (regur) or well-drained loamy soil.
- Optimal temperature: 21°C to 30°C.
- Requires 50-100 cm annual rainfall or supplementary irrigation.

## Sowing & Spacing
- Sowing Season: May to June (with pre-monsoon irrigation) or June to July (rainfed).
- Spacing: 90 cm x 60 cm for Bt Cotton varieties.

## Fertilizer Recommendation (NPK)
- Nitrogen (N): 100-120 kg/ha (split application at sowing, square formation, and flowering).
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 50-60 kg/ha (full basal dose at sowing).
- Potassium (K2O): 50-60 kg/ha (basal and flowering stage).

## Common Pests & Control
- Pink Bollworm / American Bollworm: Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 4g/10L water or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 3ml/10L.
- Sucking Pests (Aphids, Jassids): Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 3ml/10L water.
