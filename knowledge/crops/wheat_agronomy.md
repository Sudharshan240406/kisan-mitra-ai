# Agronomic Guide for Wheat Cultivation in North India

## Sowing & Water Management
For optimal wheat crop yield in Punjab, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh:
- **Sowing Window:** November 1 to November 25 is ideal.
- **Seed Rate:** 100 kg/ha for timely sowing; 125 kg/ha for late sowing.
- **Irrigation:** Wheat requires 4 to 6 irrigations depending on soil type. Crown root initiation (CRI) stage at 20-25 days after sowing is critical for first irrigation.

## Fertilizer Recommendations
- **NPK Ratio:** 120:60:40 kg/ha.
- **Application Split:** Apply full dose of Phosphorus and Potassium plus 1/3 Nitrogen at sowing. Apply remaining Nitrogen in two equal splits at first irrigation (CRI stage) and jointing stage.
