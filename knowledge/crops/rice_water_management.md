# Rice Crop Water Requirement and Irrigation Management

## Water Requirement
Paddy / Rice requires approximately 1200 mm to 1500 mm of water throughout its growing season.

## Irrigation Best Practices
1. **Initial Standing Water:** Maintain 2-5 cm of standing water during transplanting and tillering stage (first 3-4 weeks).
2. **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD):** After tillering, adopt AWD method. Allow water to recede 5 cm below soil surface before irrigating again. This saves 25-30% water without reducing yield.
3. **Critical Growth Stages:** Ensure adequate water during flowering and panicle initiation. Critical water deficit at flowering can reduce yield by up to 50%.
4. **Drainage:** Drain standing water 10-14 days before harvest to facilitate uniform grain ripening and harvesting.
